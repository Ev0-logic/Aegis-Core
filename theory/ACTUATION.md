# Phase 1: Fluid-Dynamic Actuation

## 1. MRF Interface Logic
The system utilizes Magnetorheological Fluid (MRF) as the primary actuation medium. By modulating current density, the fluid transitions between Newtonian and non-Newtonian states within <5ms.

## 2. Haptic Transparency Loop
- **Low-Viscosity State:** Zero resistance during natural movement.
- **High-Viscosity State:** Instantaneous structural support for load-bearing or impact dampening.
- **Feedback Logic:** Haptic impulses are injected directly into the MRF-matrix to simulate tactile textures.

## 3. Power Distribution
Voltage is prioritized for the localized knee and elbow actuators, ensuring gait stability even under critical power scenarios.
