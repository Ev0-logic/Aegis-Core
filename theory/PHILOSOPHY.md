# Phase 3: The Sovereignty Paradox

## 1. Agency vs. Actuation
Traditional exoskeletons treat the human as a "passenger" or a "motor." Aegis redefines this: the machine acts as an auxiliary nervous system. When the limb moves, it is not the machine *moving* the human, but the human's *will* manifesting through a fluid interface.

## 2. The Transparent Boundary
The goal of the 0x72BPM sync is the dissolution of the "interface." If the pilot can feel the weight of the machine, the system has failed. True symbiosis exists only when the machine becomes cognitively transparent.

## 3. Political Sovereignty
By decentralized hosting (Ev0-logic), the technology remains outside the control of centralized authorities. 
- **Privacy:** Neural data is purged at 0x72BPM intervals.
- **Freedom:** The pilot remains the ultimate arbiter of movement ($\Gamma_{human} \ge 0.2$).
